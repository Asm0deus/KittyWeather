Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Kitty Weather ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App").

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your unique identifier and optionally your name and email address (depending on your privacy settings with the provider).
  • Game Progress: We store your in-game progress, including cat levels, purchased items, achievements, and virtual currency balances.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: We collect device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for analytics and crash reporting.
  • Usage Data: We track app usage patterns, feature interactions, and session duration to improve user experience.
Note on Device Identifiers & Tracking: These identifiers and usage data are processed solely for internal performance monitoring, product development, and app stability. We do not use this data for cross-site tracking, nor do we share or sell it to any third-party advertising or data brokerage networks.
  • Location Data (Optional): If you grant permission, we use your approximate location to provide local weather forecasts. This data is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the App's core functionality (weather forecasts, game progress synchronization).
  • To authenticate your identity and sync progress across multiple devices.
  • To analyze app performance, diagnose software bugs, and fix technical issues.
  • To send local notifications about your virtual pet's needs (only if you explicitly enable notifications).

Third-Party Services

To ensure high performance, stability, and convenient infrastructure, we utilize the following third-party services. Please review their respective privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google LLC): Used for secure user authentication, cloud database hosting, and automated crash reporting. Privacy Policy
  • Open-Meteo API: Used as our global weather data provider. Privacy Policy
  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap): Used for secure geocoding to resolve location-based weather data. Privacy Policy
  • Unity IAP: Used exclusively for secure processing of in-app purchases. Privacy Policy

Data Storage and Security

Your game progress and account information are stored securely on Firebase cloud servers. We implement industry-standard administrative and technical security measures to safeguard your data. However, please note that no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your account data for as long as your account remains active. You have full control over your data and can request complete deletion of your account and all associated personal information at any time. This can be initiated in two ways:

  • In-App Menu: Navigate to the App's settings menu and choose the "Delete Account" option.
  • Direct Request: Send an explicit email request to our support team at black4x.dev@gmail.com, and we will purge your data from our systems manually.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights regarding your information:

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you.
  • The right to request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • The right to request total deletion of your account and data.
  • The right to opt-out of local push notifications at any time.
  • The right to revoke location permissions at any time through your iOS device system settings.

Children's Privacy

The App features family-friendly content and is suitable for all ages. It is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without parental consent. If you have reason to believe that a child under 13 has provided personal data to us, please contact us immediately, and we will take swift action to delete such data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any modifications by posting the revised document on this webpage and updating the "Last updated" date at the top.
