Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Kitty Weather ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App").

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your unique identifier and optionally your name and email address (depending on your privacy settings with the provider).
  • Game Progress: We store your in-game progress, including cat levels, purchased items, achievements, and virtual currency balances.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: We collect device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for analytics and crash reporting.
  • Usage Data: We track app usage patterns, feature interactions, and session duration to improve user experience.
  • Location Data (Optional): If you grant permission, we use your approximate location to provide local weather forecasts. This data is not stored on our servers.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the App's core functionality (weather forecasts, game progress synchronization)
  • To authenticate your identity and sync progress across devices
  • To analyze app performance and fix technical issues
  • To send local notifications about your virtual pet's needs (only if you enable notifications)

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google LLC): Authentication, cloud database, and crash reporting. Privacy Policy
  • Open-Meteo API: Weather data provider. Privacy Policy
  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap): Geocoding for location-based weather. Privacy Policy
  • Unity IAP: In-app purchase processing. Privacy Policy

Data Storage and Security

Your game progress and account information are stored securely on Firebase servers. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Data Retention

We retain your account data as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time through the App's settings menu ("Delete Account" option).

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt-out of notifications at any time
  • Revoke location permissions through your device settings

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
